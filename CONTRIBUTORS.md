# Contributing guidelines

I am open to contributions, but as a maintainer I wish to keep understanding
things and I do not want the task to become excessively difficult, as might
happen if I were to just accept and merge anything without understanding and
reviewing.

The guidelines here are meant to make my life easier, but also set the
expectations.

## What you should expect

Should you choose to contribute, please understand that I may not have the time
to review changes and might end up never merging the pull request. In some case
I may not even answer. This also applies to issues, problems or bugs.

I may politely refuse the pull request, or may request some changes. If this
offends or annoys you, please just fork the project. We may have different
visions regarding what should be added and how, and that's fine.

But you can always contact me first by e-mail to discuss the changes you would
be making, to see if it's something I would be willing to consider for merging.

## What I expect

Polite communication and behavior, as would be expected in a professional
context.

No "AI" generated code, no "AI" assistance/co-authoring, and I want to
communicate with YOU, not some chatbot. (See also
[NO-AI-POLICY.md](NO-AI-POLICY.md))

If your contribution includes code from other project, please mention it and
make sure that the licenses are compatible beforehand.

Please follow the existing indentation style and coding style as much as
possible. I understand that I am not always perfectly consistent in style
depending on context, so I am reasonably flexible. But pull requests obfuscated
with a lot of unnecessary changes to the indentation of existing code, or
insertions in totally different style (eg: spaces instead of tabs) in the
middle of a file are unacceptable.
